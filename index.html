About

I'm Serkebirhan Mosisa Beyne, a 19-year-old video editor and motion graphics designer based in Addis Ababa. I'm passionate about visual storytelling and creating content that holds attention, whether that's a promo video, a motion graphics package, or a short-form piece built for social feeds.

My work spans promo videos, motion graphics, and color grading, edited primarily in Kdenlive and CapCut. I lean on AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Gamma to speed up research, scripting, and client-facing decks, so more of my time goes into the actual craft of the edit.
